## Platform

web

## Users

Two sides of the same directory:

- **Store owners/staff in Baku**: barbershops, salons, spas, tutoring centers, gyms. Currently run bookings informally over WhatsApp; this replaces that with phone-verified accounts, staff calendars, and confirmed bookings. Admins manage services, staff, and hours; staff manage only their own calendar.
- **Consumers in Baku** (plus some tourists without a local phone number): looking for a specific business or a specific professional they already see, browsing a directory of businesses to find and book. Book via a public store page, either found through the directory or shared directly (e.g. a business's Instagram bio link).

## Product Purpose

A multi-tenant booking platform and public directory for Baku service businesses. Gives store owners phone-verified accounts, staff calendars with real availability (weekly hours + one-off/annual exceptions), and SMS-confirmed bookings. Gives consumers a central place to find a business and book a real open slot, no back-and-forth over WhatsApp. Success is store owners trusting it enough to move their real booking flow off WhatsApp, and consumers finding and completing a booking without confusion.

## Positioning

Purpose-built for how Baku service businesses actually operate today (WhatsApp-based, phone-first, cash/in-person payment, price sometimes only discussed in-store) rather than a generic Western booking SaaS template. Phone-OTP is the primary login because that's the identity SMS reminders already go to and how these businesses already communicate with clients. A neighboring generic scheduling tool could not truthfully claim the same fit to this market's habits (phone-first identity, "price discussed in store" as a first-class option, staff-level bookability tied to actually-set hours).

## Operating Context

- Store admin/staff work from a dashboard: bookings queue (needs-response / needs-completion / upcoming / past), staff roster, per-staff calendar with color-coded time-off, service list, and analytics (revenue from actually-charged amounts, top repeat customers).
- Consumers browse the homepage directory (search + category filter), land on a public store page at `/{slug}`, book a service against real open slots, and manage bookings from `/account` (upcoming, favorites, past, reschedule/cancel).
- Declined/accepted bookings surface as an unread-style notification to the consumer until they open that tab.
- **SMS (`src/lib/sms.ts`) has a real Jasmin gateway provider implemented** (`JasminSmsProvider`), active only when `JASMIN_HTTP_URL`, `JASMIN_USERNAME`, and `JASMIN_PASSWORD` are all set — unset, it stays the console stub. Sends through a self-hosted Jasmin SMS gateway's (`github.com/jookies/jasmin`) HTTP API plugin (a plain `GET /send` request), not SMPP directly; Jasmin returns HTTP 200 even on failure, with the real result in the response body ("Success ..." vs an error string), so the provider checks the body, not just the status code. A coworker is standing up the actual Jasmin server; nothing else needs to change here once it exists, just fill in the three env vars. **Email (`src/lib/email.ts`) has a real Resend provider implemented** (`ResendEmailProvider`, `resend` npm package), active only when `RESEND_API_KEY` and `RESEND_FROM_EMAIL` are both set in the environment — unset, it stays the console stub, so nothing changes until those are configured. `RESEND_FROM_EMAIL` needs a domain verified in the Resend dashboard.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 (App Router) + TypeScript, Tailwind + shadcn/ui (Base UI primitives), Postgres via Prisma 6, iron-session cookies, zod validation.
- Phone-first OTP auth; email-OTP is the fallback identity, mainly for tourists booking as consumers.
- A staff member is only bookable once they have actual working hours set; being an admin/owner does not make someone bookable by itself.
- Only one logo image exists per store today (`storage/logos/{id}`, served via `src/app/api/logos/[storeId]/route.ts`); there is no multi-photo gallery yet. This design pass adds one: business owners will be able to upload multiple photos of their business, cycling as a carousel on their homepage directory card, framed like scanning listings on a map app to pick the right business.
- Instagram-style account-creation step is built: `/profile-setup` collects a unique username and an optional profile photo, and every login redirects a username-less account there first (`resolvePostLoginRedirect` in `src/app/login/actions.ts`) before continuing to its normal destination. Username and avatar are editable afterward from `/account`.
- The main directory's combined filter button is built (`src/components/directory-filters.tsx`, one popover, Wolt-style): sort by newest, closest-to-you (browser geolocation + Haversine distance), or best reviews, plus a category filter, all in one control instead of three.
- **Rating/review system is built, with equal treatment for every rating (no gating).** A rater always picks 1-5 stars plus an optional comment on the store detail page (`src/components/review-form.tsx`), gated on having an actual `COMPLETED` booking there — no open review solicitation. Every rating, regardless of the number, is saved the same way and is visible to that store's admins at `/admin/[slug]/reviews` (rating, comment, reviewer name/phone/email) and counts toward the store's public average/count. Posting the same rating to Google is a separate, always-available choice — after submitting, everyone sees an identical "Also post this rating on Google" link (using `Store.googleReviewUrl`, set by the owner in `/admin/[slug]/settings`) if the store has one configured, offered regardless of how positive the rating was, never routed by sentiment. An earlier version of this feature routed 4-5 star ratings to Google automatically while keeping 1-3 star ratings private-only ("review gating," which Google's review policies generally prohibit) — removed at the user's request in favor of this equal-treatment version.
- **Multi-business ownership is built.** `createStoreAction` (`src/app/onboarding/actions.ts`) no longer blocks a second `ADMIN` membership — `/onboarding` always shows the create-a-business form (labeled "Add another business" with existing businesses listed for context, once the account already owns one), and the dashboard nav's "Add another business" link is always visible, not just for accounts with zero businesses. `BusinessSwitcher` already supported multiple `ADMIN` memberships before this, so switching between owned businesses needed no changes. Monthly-fee-per-business is explicitly deferred — no payment/billing work has been done.
- **Same-name-business disambiguation is built** (`src/lib/duplicate-name.ts`), including the residual "both blank" case. `Store.phone` is now a real, owner-settable field (onboarding + `/admin/[slug]/settings`, icon-prefixed input, shown on the public store page) — it existed in the schema unused before this. The directory's disambiguator (`store-panel.tsx`'s `disambiguatorLine`) cascades: address if set, else phone if set, else the store's own unique slug (`@store-slug`, monospace, since it's the one field guaranteed different between any two stores) — so two same-named businesses are never both showing identical, non-distinguishing text, even when neither has an address. At creation/rename time, the nudge toast (`onboarding/actions.ts`, `admin/[slug]/settings/actions.ts`) now only fires when *both* address and phone are blank, since either one alone already solves it.
- **Calendar export is built** (`src/lib/calendar-links.ts`, `src/components/add-to-calendar.tsx`), shown on a fresh booking confirmation and on any confirmed upcoming booking in `/account`: "Add to Google Calendar" (a direct `calendar.google.com` link, no file) and "Add to Apple Calendar" (`/api/bookings/[bookingId]/calendar.ics`, served `Content-Type: text/calendar` with no forced download so iOS Safari opens the native Add Event sheet). Both carry a reminder fixed at 8:00 AM Baku time on the day of the appointment (not "N hours before"), with the event title, time, and store address/location — the ICS `VALARM` uses an absolute `TRIGGER;VALUE=DATE-TIME`, which Google's simple render-URL flow has no equivalent for, so a Google Calendar add falls back to that account's own default reminder setting; only the Apple/ICS path carries the custom morning alarm. **Note**: `Booking.startAt/endAt` are UTC-labeled timestamps whose digits actually represent Baku wall-clock time (see `src/lib/schedule.ts`), assuming the server process runs with `TZ=UTC`; `calendar-links.ts` corrects for this with a fixed AZT (UTC+4, no DST) offset before emitting real-world-accurate times to Google/Apple — re-verify this conversion if the deployment's server timezone assumption ever changes.
- The homepage directory map (`DirectoryMap`) is now always rendered, even with zero results (e.g. an empty search) or globally zero stores — it previously disappeared entirely in those cases; the empty/no-match message now renders inside the list column only, never replacing the whole `DirectoryView`.
- **Mobile navigation dead-ends fixed.** Tapping Bookings/Favorites/Profile in `MobileTabBar` while logged out (or the old homepage "Run a business" link) sent a guest to `/login` with no way back, since the bottom tab bar itself is hidden on `/login` — `src/app/login/back-link.tsx` adds a "Continue browsing" control (`router.back()`, falling back to `/` with no history) above the sign-in card. The header's "Log in" button (`site-header.tsx`) was `variant="ghost"` and barely readable; it's now `variant="default"` (solid madder-red), the one real CTA a guest sees. The Search tab (`/?focus=search`) silently did nothing on a second tap because `autoFocus` only fires on mount and tapping it while already on `/` is a same-route soft navigation reusing the mounted component; `store-search-form.tsx` now re-focuses via a ref + effect keyed on the `autoFocusSearch` prop, so every transition into `focus=search` refocuses regardless of whether the component remounted. The homepage's "Run a business in Baku?" footer link is gone entirely, both because it forced login with no way back and because it wouldn't stay visible/discoverable once the directory has hundreds of listings; the same ask now lives permanently in `/account`'s Profile tab.
- **Map popup CTA fixed.** "Open salon page" assumed every listing was a salon; the directory includes barbershops, gyms, tutoring centers, spas, etc., so it's now "Open business page." Separately, the button's text was unreadable (dark text on the solid madder-red background) — an earlier fix for Leaflet's own anchor-color override (`color: inherit !important`, needed so Leaflet's default blue link style didn't win) had also overridden this button's intended ivory text color, since both rules target the same element. Scoped a more specific `.schedule-map-popup-cta` override so this button's text stays `--primary-foreground` regardless.
- **Booking confirmation calendar buttons centered.** "Add to Google Calendar"/"Add to Apple Calendar" (`add-to-calendar.tsx`) now use `justify-center` and the wrapping element is `w-full`, so they center against the full "Request sent" card width instead of just the tight-wrapped content box.
- **Mobile header overflow fixed.** `SiteHeader` (consumer pages) at 390px and below, logged in as a business owner, overflowed the viewport — "Business dashboard" + "My account" + "Log out" didn't fit next to the wordmark, visually colliding with it. "My account"/"Log out" are now hidden under `sm:` (redundant with the bottom tab bar's Profile tab, which already has both), the dashboard button shows "Dashboard" on mobile, and the logo got `shrink-0` so it can never be squeezed. `DashboardNav` (admin/staff) had the same problem for a staff member who also owns another business — three competing links/switcher on one row with no wrap; that row is now `flex-wrap`, with shortened mobile labels ("← Schedule.az", "Add business"/"Start a business", "Switch") for the same reason.
- **Directory map popup: image carousel + button fix.** The map pin popup only ever showed one static photo with no way to browse a business's other uploaded images; `directory-map.tsx` now takes the store's full `images` array and renders the same hover-style prev/next arrows and dot indicators as the directory card and store gallery. Separately, "Open salon page" inside the popup rendered as a plain blue underlined hyperlink instead of the intended solid button — Leaflet's own stylesheet styles every anchor inside `.leaflet-container` and loads after ours, beating the Tailwind color/underline utilities; fixed with a scoped `.schedule-map-popup a` override in `globals.css`.
- **Copy cleanup (no-ai-writing pass).** Removed the "Visitor/traffic analytics aren't tracked yet..." paragraph from the admin overview page entirely (also flagged as AI-sounding hedge text). Rewrote the Google-review settings copy (`google-review-form.tsx`) to drop an em dash and the "offered equally regardless of the rating, never routed by" phrasing. ## Brand Commitments

- Name: Schedule.az. No logo/wordmark asset exists yet (`public/` only has unused default Next.js starter SVGs) — the product identity is effectively unset going into this design pass.
- No existing visual identity is binding. The current UI is the stock shadcn/base-ui default theme (green/gray, Geist Sans), produced by an earlier AI pass with no deliberate design decisions behind it. Treat it as evidence of layout/functionality only, not as an aesthetic to preserve. `CLAUDE.md`'s design policy names a warm amber/terracotta palette that was never actually implemented anywhere in the code; it's a stale/aspirational note from that same earlier pass, not a constraint.
- UI copy is English only for this pass. Store names, categories, and descriptions are often Azerbaijani or Russian (Cyrillic + Azerbaijani diacritics), entered by real business owners — typography must render both cleanly even though UI chrome stays English.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real store photos exist yet; the gallery/carousel feature ships ahead of that content. Card design for a store with zero uploaded photos must not fabricate imagery (no stock photos, no generic illustration standing in for a real business) — falls back to the existing logo/initials avatar treatment, same as today, until the owner uploads at least 2 photos.
- No real user testimonials, usage stats, or customer logos exist. None may be invented for any surface.

## Product Principles

1. Fit Baku's actual business habits (phone-first, in-person price/payment norms) over copying generic Western SaaS booking-tool conventions.
2. Never fabricate content: no invented stats, testimonials, stock photography standing in for a real business, or placeholder-shaped copy that isn't true yet.
3. The directory's homepage is a discovery surface, not just a list: it should let a consumer visually recognize and compare real businesses (photos, name, what they do) the way a map app's place cards do, to make picking the right one easier.
4. Only bookable, real capacity is shown to consumers (a staff member with no set hours isn't presented as available).
5. The design system established now must scale cleanly to many more stores and future admin/staff surfaces, not just look right on the one homepage it starts with.
